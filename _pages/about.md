<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [The University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk/), supervised by [Prof. Kenneth K.Y. Wong](https://i.cs.hku.hk/~kykwong/). My research focuses on high-quality and efficient visual content generation, with a particular interest in diffusion-based video generation, acceleration, and restoration.

Before joining HKU, I received my M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees in Computer Science from [Harbin Institute of Technology](https://www.hit.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Wangmeng Zuo](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/wangmengzuo). I have also worked closely with [Prof. Chenyang Si](https://chenyangsi.top/) on visual content generation.
